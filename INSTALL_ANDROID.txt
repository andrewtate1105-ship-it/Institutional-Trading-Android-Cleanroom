ANDROID INSTALLATION

1. Download Institutional-Trading-System-Android.apk to your Android phone.
2. Open the Files app or Downloads folder and tap the APK.
3. If Android asks, allow installation from this browser or file manager.
4. Tap Install, then open Institutional Trading System.
5. Choose Stocks or F&O mode.
6. Enter the stock/index name or NSE symbol and the trading timeframe (5M, 15M, 1H, D or W).
7. Tap RUN INSTITUTIONAL ANALYSIS.
8. Review LONG / SHORT / NO TRADE, entry, structural stop, targets and higher-timeframe confirmation shown inside the app.

The app is signal-only and does not place broker orders. It does not require Telegram or broker credentials. F&O mode will not invent contract strikes, premiums, OI, IV or Greeks when the active market-data provider does not supply verified derivative data. Software completion does not establish profitability or statistical validity.
