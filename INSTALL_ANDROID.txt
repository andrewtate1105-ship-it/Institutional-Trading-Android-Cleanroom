ANDROID INSTALLATION

1. Download Institutional-Trading-System-Android.apk to your Android phone.
2. Open the Files app or your Downloads folder.
3. Tap the APK file.
4. Allow installation from unknown apps for the browser or file manager when Android asks.
5. Tap Install, then open Institutional Trading System.
6. Enter the Telegram bot token only inside the app.
7. Enter the private Telegram chat ID, account equity, risk percentage, watchlist, and timeframes.
8. Tap Send Telegram test.
9. Never enter a Groww password or any broker credentials.

The app is signal-only and does not place orders. Software completion does not establish profitability or statistical validity.
